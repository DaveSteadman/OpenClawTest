GBSR is a mobile radar system that functions in two main capacities: Surveillance and Fall of Shot (FoS) correction. In its Surveillance role, the system is capable of tracking and classifying personnel, vehicles, helicopters, and drones – thereby offering troops a precise overview of allied movements and an early alert regarding potential threats. For FoS correction, GBSR assists mortar and artillery units by providing swift fire adjustments through the detection and localisation of rounds with a high degree of accuracy, thereby enhancing the effectiveness and lethality of fire teams.
