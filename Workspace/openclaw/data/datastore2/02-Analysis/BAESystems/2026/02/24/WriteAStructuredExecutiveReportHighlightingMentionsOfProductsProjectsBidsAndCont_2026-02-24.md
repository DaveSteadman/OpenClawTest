## 2. Products and Projects

BAE Systems continues to advance a portfolio of advanced defense platforms, including the Paladin Self‑Propelled Howitzer, which received a $500 million contract in December 2025 to support the U.S. Army’s Armored Brigade Combat Teams. The company is also engaged in the development of the Advanced Precision Kill Weapon System (APKWS) upgrade, a dual‑mode air‑to‑air weapon that integrates a long‑wave infrared seeker and a mid‑body warhead to counter unmanned aerial systems. In the aviation sector, BAE is building 20 Eurofighter Typhoon aircraft for Turkey under a four‑billion‑euro contract, with deliveries slated to begin in 2030. Additionally, BAE’s Information and Electronic Systems Integration division secured a $98.9 million contract for F‑16 sustainment services, underscoring its role in maintaining U.S. Air Force avionics.

## 3. Contract Awards

In 2025, BAE Systems amassed over £27 billion ($35 billion) in contracts across aviation, naval, ground, and electronic systems, reflecting robust demand from NATO modernization programmes. The company’s contract portfolio includes a $1.7 billion five‑year U.S. Navy agreement for laser‑guidance kits that convert unguided munitions into precision‑guided weapons. The U.S. Air Force awarded a $145 million sole‑source contract to BAE for the APKWS upgrade, with work slated to commence in the second quarter of fiscal year 2026. These awards demonstrate BAE’s continued penetration into key U.S. defense procurement streams.

## 4. Financial Performance

BAE Systems reported underlying earnings before interest and taxes of £3.32 billion for 2025, a 12% increase over 2024, while sales rose 10% to a record £30.66 billion. The order backlog reached a record £83.6 billion at the end of December, with order intake of £36.8 billion. The company forecasts underlying earnings growth of 9% to 11% and sales growth of 7% to 9% for 2026, signalling confidence in sustaining momentum amid heightened global defense spending.

## 5. Summary

BAE Systems’ recent product launches and contract wins underscore its strategic position as Europe’s largest defense contractor and a key partner for U.S. military modernization. The company’s portfolio spans advanced artillery, fighter aircraft, missile systems, and electronic warfare solutions, supported by substantial contract awards totaling tens of billions of dollars. Financial results reveal record sales and a growing backlog, while the company projects continued growth in the coming year. These developments collectively affirm BAE Systems’ capacity to deliver cutting‑edge defense capabilities and maintain a strong market presence.
