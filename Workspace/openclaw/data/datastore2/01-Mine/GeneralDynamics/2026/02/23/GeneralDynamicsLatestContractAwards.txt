The contract follows Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement provisions governing obligations ahead of fiscal year 2026 funding availability. Seventy percent of the work will be performed in Groton, Connecticut, with the remainder in Newport News, Virginia, and Quonset Point, Rhode Island. The Navy anticipates the project will be completed by December 2031.
