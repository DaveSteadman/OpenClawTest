The nation's parliamentary democracy operates through the Storting, with Prime Minister Jonas Gahr Støre leading a Labour minority government following parliamentary elections in September 2025. The Government Pension Fund Global holds assets exceeding $1.9 trillion, equivalent to over $340,000 per Norwegian citizen.
