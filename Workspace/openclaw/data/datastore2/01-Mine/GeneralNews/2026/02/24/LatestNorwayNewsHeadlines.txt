Norway is a prosperous Nordic country with a population of approximately 5.7 million, situated on the Scandinavian Peninsula between the North Atlantic and the Arctic. The capital Oslo is home to over 1.1 million people, while other major cities include Bergen and Trondheim, each steeped in centuries of history. The nation's parliamentary democracy operates through the Storting, with Prime Minister Jonas Gahr Støre leading a Labour minority government following parliamentary elections in September 2025. The Norwegian economy is underpinned by its massive oil and gas industry, with state revenues from petroleum activities funding the world's largest sovereign wealth fund. The Government Pension Fund Global holds assets exceeding $1.9 trillion, equivalent to over $340,000 per Norwegian citizen. Political debate centres on how much to spend from the fund versus saving for future generations, energy policy, and maintaining Norway's role as a stable energy supplier to Europe while pursuing climate commitments. Norway's dramatic landscape of fjords, mountains, and coastal waters has shaped both its history and contemporary identity.
